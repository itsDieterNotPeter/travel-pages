Penang · George Town UNESCO Heritage Zone

Street Art Walking Route

All within about 1.5km, flat and walkable

George Town's street art is concentrated in a ~1.5km loop around Armenian Street, Lebuh Cannon, Lebuh Ah Quee and Muntri Street. Most are Ernest Zacharevic originals from the 2012 George Town Festival, restored in 2024. Some have faded or disappeared entirely.

Clusters are ordered west to east. Walk between them at your own pace, not in strict sequence. Pick what interests you in each area.

Good to know

📍 52 steel rod sculptures scattered across the heritage zone. Free map from Penang Tourism office on Lebuh Pantai.

🕐 Best light is early morning (7-9 AM) or late afternoon (4-6 PM). Midday sun washes out the colours.

🚶 The whole route is about 1.5km of flat walking. Allow 2-3 hours with photo stops.

☕ Armenian Street has the most cafes if you need a break between clusters.

📸 Children on a Bicycle gets crowded by 10 AM. Go early if you want a clean shot.
